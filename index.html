KlipBot

HomeTerms of ServicePrivacy Policy

KlipBot is a personal automation tool. It finds trending moments in public live streams on Twitch, Kick and YouTube, cuts short vertical clips, adds subtitles and a short comment, and publishes them to the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

How it uses platform APIs

  • YouTube Data API: find live streams and upload videos, titles, translated metadata and caption tracks to the operator's channel.
  • Instagram API: publish Reels to the operator's professional account and read its audience insights.
  • TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API: sign in with the operator's TikTok account and post videos to that account.

Every clip credits the original streamer and links to the source stream.

Content removal

Creators who want a clip removed, or who do not want their streams clipped, can open a request at https://github.com/tahademirkama-svg/klipbot/issues. Removed creators are added to a blocklist.
